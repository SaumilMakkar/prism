## 1. Screen issues

### 1.1 Screen won't respond to touch
If the screen does not respond to touch, first perform a soft restart by holding the Power and Volume Down buttons for 10 seconds. If the device does not restart, connect it to a charger for 15 minutes and try again — a fully drained battery can appear as an unresponsive screen.

### 1.2 Screen flickers or shows lines
Flickering or vertical lines usually indicate a display connector issue after a drop or water exposure. This cannot be resolved by software reset; the device must be sent to a service centre for a hardware inspection.

## 2. Power issues

### 2.1 Device will not power on
If the device will not power on, connect the original charger and cable and wait 10 minutes before attempting to power on — some units require a minimum charge threshold before the power-on sequence will complete. If the device still will not power on after 10 minutes of charging, hold Power and Volume Down for 15 seconds to force a hardware reset. If there is still no response, the battery or power IC may have failed and the device should be taken to an authorized service centre.

### 2.2 Device powers on but restarts repeatedly (boot loop)
A boot loop is most commonly caused by a corrupted software update. Boot into Safe Mode by holding Volume Down during the Samsung logo screen, then check whether the device stabilizes. If it does, uninstall the most recently installed app. If the boot loop persists in Safe Mode, a factory reset via Recovery Mode is required after backing up data.

## 3. Charging issues

### 3.1 Device charges slowly
Slow charging is usually caused by using a non-original cable or a USB port instead of a wall adapter. Confirm the charger outputs at least 25W for fast-charging-capable models. Debris in the charging port can also reduce contact — inspect and clean the port with a soft brush before replacing any cable.

### 3.2 Device does not charge at all
If the device shows no charging indicator at all, test with a different cable and adapter first. If charging still fails, the charging port itself may need cleaning or replacement at a service centre.
