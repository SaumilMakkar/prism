## 6. Initial setup

### 6.1 Installing the app
Download SmartThings from the Galaxy Store or Google Play. Sign in with the same Samsung account used on the phone so devices sync automatically across the ecosystem.

### 6.2 Adding a new device
Open SmartThings, tap the "+" icon, and select "Add device." Choose the device category (light, plug, sensor, etc.) and follow the in-app pairing instructions. Most devices must be in pairing mode (usually a held button press) before they appear in the scan.

## 7. Connectivity

### 7.1 Device shows offline
If a paired device shows offline, check that the SmartThings Hub (if used) has power and a stable internet connection. For Wi-Fi-only devices, confirm the device is on the same 2.4GHz network as the hub — most SmartThings devices do not support 5GHz networks.

### 7.2 Removing a device
To remove a device, long-press its tile in the SmartThings app, select the device, tap the three-dot menu, then "Remove device." This does not delete any automations that referenced it; those must be edited separately.
