## 4. Warranty coverage

### 4.1 Standard warranty period
All Galaxy devices purchased through authorized retailers carry a 12-month standard warranty covering manufacturing defects, extended to 24 months for devices registered within 30 days of purchase via the Samsung Members app.

### 4.2 Devices purchased abroad
Effective this version, devices purchased abroad are covered under the same 12-month (or 24-month registered) warranty as domestic purchases, provided the original purchase receipt is presented. An international warranty card is no longer required. Coverage still excludes accidental damage.

## 5. Returns

### 5.1 Return window
Devices may be returned within 14 days of purchase for a full refund, provided the device is unused and in original packaging. This extends the previous 7-day window.

### 5.2 Exchange for defective units
A device with a manufacturing defect confirmed within the return window may be exchanged for a new unit at no cost, without needing to go through the standard warranty repair process.
