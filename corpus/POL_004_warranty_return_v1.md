## 4. Warranty coverage

### 4.1 Standard warranty period
All Galaxy devices purchased through authorized retailers carry a 12-month standard warranty covering manufacturing defects.

### 4.2 Devices purchased abroad
Devices purchased outside the country of use are covered under warranty only if the original purchase receipt and an international warranty card are presented. Coverage is limited to manufacturing defects and does not include accidental damage.

## 5. Returns

### 5.1 Return window
Devices may be returned within 7 days of purchase for a full refund, provided the device is unused and in original packaging.
