## 8. Repair turnaround

### 8.1 Standard repair SLA
Standard in-warranty repairs (screen, battery, charging port) are completed within 3 business days of device drop-off at an authorized service centre.

### 8.2 Complex repair SLA
Repairs requiring a motherboard or camera module replacement are completed within 7 business days, subject to parts availability.

## 9. Loaner devices

### 9.1 Eligibility
A loaner device is provided free of charge for repairs expected to exceed 5 business days, for devices under active warranty or an active Samsung Care+ plan.
